## Not a VIOFO product

NECKTIED is an unofficial project and is not affiliated with VIOFO Ltd. "VIOFO" and the model names are used only to describe which cameras NECKTIED is meant for. This repository contains no VIOFO firmware and no parts of it. Every user downloads the original firmware from VIOFO (<https://www.viofo.com/pages/firmware>); the patcher modifies it only on the user's own computer.

## Liability

Modifying camera firmware is at your own risk. Flashing modified firmware always carries a risk of making the camera unusable ("bricking"), and it may void the warranty and any claims against VIOFO. NECKTIED and its contributors accept no liability for damaged cameras or lost claims. See `LICENSE` (provided "as is", without warranty).

## Time zone data

`assets/necktied_tz.bin` is a simplified raster derived from
[timezone-boundary-builder](https://github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder),
whose data is based on [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
The derived raster is also available under the ODbL 1.0.
